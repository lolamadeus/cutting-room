How it works

Review: go through each clip. Drag along the lane under the waveform to mark the bits worth using, tag each one, add notes, then give the clip a priority. Everything saves automatically.

Board: every highlight, grouped by category. Drag cards to reorder them or move them to another category, and drag a category's header to reorder the sections. That order is the rough running order. Play rough cut plays them back to back, and the total is checked against the target length.
