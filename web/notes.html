Render notes

Watch a render and leave notes for the next round. Each note remembers which segment of the cut it's on and where that is in the source clip, so the next version can act on it. Notes save on their own to edit/render-notes.json.

SpacePlay / pause (or click the video)
NNote at the playhead (pauses and opens the note)
I then ONote on a stretch: in at I, out at O. Or drag along the notes lane
← →Back / forward 1 s (with Shift: 5 s)
, .One frame back / forward
[ ]Previous / next segment
PPlay the selected note's stretch
DelDelete the selected note
EscLeave the text box
